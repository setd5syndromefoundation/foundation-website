Open the minutes form

Pre-fill the Meeting Details section

During the Meeting
1
Attendance
  • Click each board member's name to mark them Present or Absent
  • The form tracks the quorum count automatically
  • Add any guests or observers in the Guests / Observers field
  • Under "Quorum established?" select Yes if 4 or more members are present
2
Approval of Agenda

Kate will ask for a motion to approve the agenda. Record:

  • Who makes the motion
  • Who seconds it
  • The result — select "Approved unanimously" if no objections
3
Approval of Prior Minutes

The board will vote to approve the minutes from the April 29, 2026 inaugural meeting.

  • Prior Meeting Date: April 29, 2026
  • Record who moves to approve and who seconds
  • Record the result — likely "Approved unanimously"
  • If anyone notes a correction, write it in the Notes or Corrections field
4
Old Business
  • There is no old business for this meeting — leave this section blank
5
New Business — Discussion Items

Click "+ Add item" for each of the three discussion items below. For all three, select "No vote taken — discussion only" as the result and leave Motion, Seconded, and Vote fields blank.

Item 1 — Website Review
  • Item name: Website review and discussion
  • Write a brief summary of the key points raised
Item 2 — Strategic Framework Draft
  • Item name: Strategic framework draft review and discussion
  • Write a brief summary of the key points raised
Item 3 — Spain Association Meeting
  • Item name: Scheduling a meeting with the Spain association
  • Write a brief summary of what was discussed
  • If a date or action is agreed to, note it here and add it to Action Items
6
Action Items

Capture any follow-up tasks assigned during the meeting. For each one:

  • Describe the action clearly
  • Record who is responsible
  • Record the due date if one was given
  • Click "+ Add action item" to add more rows
7
Adjournment

Kate will call for a motion to adjourn. Record:

  • Who moves to adjourn and who seconds
  • What time the meeting ended
  • The next meeting date and time if one was set
8
Secretary Certification
  • Type your full name in the "Secretary Signature" field at the bottom
  • Enter today's date as the Date of Certification
After the Meeting
1
Export the minutes
  • Scroll to the bottom of the form and click "Print / Export"
  • When the print dialog opens, change the destination to "Save as PDF"
  • Save the file as: Meeting Minutes 5.27.2026.pdf
2
Upload to the board portal
  • Log in to the board portal at setd5syndrome.org/board/
  • Go to Board Meeting Materials → Meeting Minutes
  • Upload the PDF you just saved
3
Distribute to the board
  • Distribute the PDF to all board members — aim to have them out within a few days of the meeting while it's still fresh
Tips
  • Minutes record what was decided and what actions were assigned — not a word-for-word transcript of the conversation.
  • For discussion items with no vote, a 2–3 sentence summary of the main points is enough.
  • If you miss something during the meeting, flag it to Kate right after and fill it in while it's fresh.
  • It's okay to write shorthand during the meeting and clean it up afterward.
