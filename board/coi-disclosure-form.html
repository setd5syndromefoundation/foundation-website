Conflict of Interest Disclosure Statement
Board of Directors  ·  Complete and submit annually or when circumstances change

A conflict of interest exists when a board member has a personal, financial, or professional interest that could compromise their objectivity or judgment in Foundation matters. If you are unsure whether something constitutes a conflict, disclose it and let the Board make that determination.

B. Disclosure of Interests

B1 — Financial Interests

B2 — Family and Personal Relationships

Do you have a family member or close personal relationship with anyone employed by, contracted with, or otherwise having a financial relationship with the Foundation?

B3 — Other Affiliations

Are you affiliated (as an officer, director, employee, or advisor) with any organization that may have interests that conflict with the Foundation?

B4 — Personal Projects and Independent Activities

Do you operate, manage, or have a material interest in any personal project, website, publication, or independent activity that relates to SETD5 Syndrome or that the Foundation may reference, link to, or consider working with?
