SETD5 Syndrome Foundation, Inc.  ·  A Virginia Nonstock Corporation
Written Consent of Remaining Initial Directors
In Lieu of Organizational Meeting  ·  Pursuant to the Foundation's Bylaws and the Virginia Nonstock Corporation Act

The undersigned, constituting all of the remaining initial directors of SETD5 Syndrome Foundation, Inc. (the "Foundation"), a Virginia nonstock corporation, hereby consent to and adopt the following resolutions by unanimous written consent in lieu of a meeting, pursuant to the Foundation's Bylaws and the Virginia Nonstock Corporation Act.

Recitals

WHEREAS, Kate Carr, Laura Biazon, and Amanda ("Mandy") Wittenberg were named as initial directors of the Foundation in the Articles of Incorporation filed with the Commonwealth of Virginia;

WHEREAS, Amanda ("Mandy") Wittenberg delivered written notice of resignation as a director prior to the organizational meeting held on April 29, 2026;

WHEREAS, Kate Carr and Laura Biazon constitute all of the remaining initial directors of the Foundation;

WHEREAS, Article II, Section 2 of the Foundation's Bylaws provides that the Board shall consist of not fewer than three (3) nor more than fifteen (15) directors, as determined by the Board from time to time by resolution, and that the founding Board shall consist of three (3) to seven (7) directors;

WHEREAS, Article II, Section 10 of the Foundation's Bylaws permits Board action to be taken without a meeting by written consent, provided the required notice, opportunity to respond, and recordkeeping requirements are satisfied;

WHEREAS, the remaining initial directors desire to formally acknowledge Amanda ("Mandy") Wittenberg's resignation, expand and fix the size of the founding Board, elect and seat the Foundation's six-person Board of Directors, and ratify and confirm the organizational actions taken at the April 29, 2026 organizational meeting;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED as follows:

Resolutions

Resolution 1 — Acknowledgment of Resignation

The remaining initial directors hereby acknowledge receipt of the written resignation of Amanda ("Mandy") Wittenberg as a director of the Foundation, effective upon delivery of her written notice of resignation unless otherwise stated in the notice.

Her resignation shall be recorded in the permanent corporate records of the Foundation.

Resolution 2 — Expansion and Fixing of Founding Board Size

Pursuant to Article II, Section 2 of the Bylaws, the remaining initial directors hereby expand and fix the number of directors constituting the founding Board of Directors at six (6).

The six-person founding Board is within the authorized founding Board range of three (3) to seven (7) directors and within the overall authorized Board range of three (3) to fifteen (15) directors.

Resolution 3 — Election and Seating of Directors

The following individuals are hereby elected and seated as Directors of SETD5 Syndrome Foundation, Inc., effective as of April 29, 2026:

Kate Carr
Laura Biazon
Samantha Cotter
Amy Kreller-Kochis
Jesiree Mendez
Taryn Mildenhall

Each director listed above shall serve in accordance with the Foundation's Articles of Incorporation and Bylaws, including any applicable staggered term provisions. Specific initial term lengths shall be determined by the Board at its next duly held meeting or by written consent.

Resolution 4 — Ratification and Confirmation of Organizational Meeting Actions

The remaining initial directors hereby ratify, confirm, and approve the organizational actions taken at the organizational meeting held on April 29, 2026, including but not limited to:

(a) adoption of the Bylaws of SETD5 Syndrome Foundation, Inc.;
(b) adoption of the Board Policies Handbook;
(c) approval of officer roles and Board leadership roles;
(d) approval, acknowledgment, or receipt of any conflict of interest disclosures, policy acknowledgments, or other governance documents presented at the meeting;
(e) approval of the meeting minutes or direction that the minutes be prepared, circulated, and approved in accordance with the Bylaws; and
(f) any other organizational actions taken at the meeting that are consistent with the Articles of Incorporation, Bylaws, Board Policies Handbook, and applicable law.

Resolution 5 — Confirmation of Officer and Board Leadership Roles

The remaining initial directors hereby ratify, confirm, and approve the officer and Board leadership roles approved at the organizational meeting held on April 29, 2026, and formally record the following officers of the Foundation:

Kate CarrPresident / Board Chair
Laura BiazonTreasurer
Samantha CotterSecretary

Each officer shall serve in accordance with Article III of the Bylaws until the next Annual Meeting of the Board or until their successor is duly elected and qualified, unless earlier resignation or removal occurs in accordance with the Bylaws.

Resolution 6 — Ratification of Prior Organizational Actions

To the extent necessary and permitted by law, all prior organizational actions taken by the remaining initial directors on behalf of the Foundation are hereby ratified, confirmed, and approved.

Resolution 7 — Corporate Records

The Secretary or other appropriate officer is directed to file this Written Consent, Amanda ("Mandy") Wittenberg's resignation notice, the adopted Bylaws, the adopted Board Policies Handbook, and the minutes of the April 29, 2026 organizational meeting in the Foundation's permanent corporate records.

This Written Consent may be executed in counterparts. Electronic or scanned signatures shall be deemed valid for all purposes under the Foundation's Bylaws and applicable law.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the undersigned remaining initial directors have executed this Written Consent as of the date of the last signature below.
