SETD5 Syndrome Foundation — Confidential

Meeting script — inaugural board meeting

April 29, 2026 · For use by the board president

This script provides exact language for running each item of the April 29th meeting. Text in the blue boxes is what you say aloud. Italicized text in gray is a stage direction or note for you only. Motion language is provided in the gold boxes — a board member other than you should make each motion.

1
Call to order and roll call
Opening
Say this

"I'd like to call this meeting to order. Today is April 29, 2026, and this is the inaugural meeting of the Board of Directors of SETD5 Syndrome Foundation, Inc. The time is 4:00 PM PST."

"I'll now take roll. Please confirm your presence when I call your name."

[Call each board member's name and note who is present. Note the meeting location or platform — e.g. Zoom, in person, etc.]

"We have [NUMBER] directors present. Our bylaws require a majority of our 6 directors for quorum — that's 4. [If quorum met:] We have quorum and may proceed with business. [If short:] We're waiting on one more director — let's give it a few minutes before we begin formal business."

Secretary: Record the date, time, platform, and names of all directors present and absent in the minutes.
2
Welcome and purpose of the meeting
Opening
Say this

"Thank you all for being here. This is a significant moment — we're formally establishing the governance foundation that will guide this organization as we work to support families affected by SETD5 syndrome and advance research into this condition."

"Today's agenda covers the adoption of our bylaws, election of officers, adoption of our board policies handbook and document retention policy, and several resolutions needed to get the Foundation operational. We'll also have time to discuss the Foundation's structure and early priorities."

"Before we begin, I want to acknowledge that we've all put a lot of thought and care into getting these documents right. I'm grateful for the conversations we've had to get here."

"One quick tech note before we dive in — throughout the meeting I'll be asking everyone to open documents from the board portal. You'll want to have a browser window open behind Zoom so you can pull those up as we go. If you haven't already, go ahead and open the board portal and navigate to the Meeting Packet so it's ready. You won't need to switch away from Zoom until I ask."

"And for anyone who hasn't served on a board before — here's how today works. I'll introduce each agenda item and open it up for discussion — so if you have questions, concerns, or want to suggest a change, that's the time to speak up. Once discussion is done, someone other than me makes a formal motion — that just means saying out loud 'I move that we adopt X.' It's how we officially put something up for a vote. Someone else then seconds it, which just means they support bringing it to a vote. From there we have a few options: we can vote to adopt it as is, vote to adopt it with an amendment if someone wants a change, or vote to table it and come back to it at a future meeting. If we do amend something, we vote on the amendment first, then vote to adopt the whole document as amended — so there will be two votes. If we can't reach agreement on something today, we table it, I'll take the feedback away, revise the document, and we'll bring it back at the next meeting. Our secretary records every vote. Any questions before we get started?"

3
Adoption of bylaws
Vote required
Say this

"Our first order of business is the adoption of the bylaws of SETD5 Syndrome Foundation, Inc. A summary of changes from the draft bylaws has been circulated. Does anyone have questions about the bylaws or the changes before we proceed?"

[Allow discussion. Answer questions. If someone raises a concern that requires a change, note it and either address it by amendment or table that section. Once discussion is complete:]

"Are we ready to vote on adopting the bylaws?"

📄 Ask everyone to open this document: "Please open the bylaws document in your board meeting packet — it's labeled 'Bylaws of SETD5 Syndrome Foundation, Inc.' Take a moment to look it over if you haven't already."
Motion language — another board member says this

"I move that the Board of Directors adopt the bylaws of SETD5 Syndrome Foundation, Inc. as presented."

After motion is seconded, say this

"The motion has been made and seconded. All in favor — please raise your hand." [Count and record hands.]

"Any opposed?" [Pause, record any.] "Any abstentions?" [Pause, record any.]

"The motion carries. The bylaws of SETD5 Syndrome Foundation, Inc. are adopted as of April 29, 2026."

Secretary: Record the vote by name. Note that the bylaws include the Conflict of Interest Policy as an appendix.
If someone disagrees or wants a change

Minor wording concern: "That's a good note. Let's add it to the list for our first amendment. Are we comfortable adopting as presented and addressing that at our next meeting?" [If yes, proceed to vote.]

Wants to change something before voting: "Would the board be willing to amend that section now? Can I get a motion to amend [Section X] to read [new language]?" [Vote on the amendment first, then vote to adopt as amended.]

Wants to table it entirely: "I hear that. Let's table the bylaws vote and schedule a working session before our next meeting to work through it." [Note: this delays everything — gently remind the board that adopted bylaws are needed to operate.]

Secretary — if an amendment is voted on: Record two separate votes in the minutes. First: "[Name] moved to amend Section X to read [new language]. Seconded by [name]. Vote: [ayes/nays]. Motion carried." Then: "[Name] moved to adopt the bylaws as amended. Seconded by [name]. Vote: [ayes/nays]. Motion carried." Do not rewrite the document — just record what changed.

4
Election of officers
Vote required
Say this

"Now that the bylaws are adopted, we need to elect our officers. Under the bylaws, the officers are the President/Board Chair, Secretary, and Treasurer. I'll open the floor for nominations for each position."

[For each officer position:]

"I'll take nominations for the position of [POSITION]. Are there any nominations?"

[Once nominations are made:] "Are there any further nominations?" [Pause.] "Hearing none, nominations are closed."

Motion language for each officer

"I move that [NAME] be elected as [POSITION] of SETD5 Syndrome Foundation, Inc."

After each motion is seconded, say this

"All in favor — please raise your hand." [Count and record hands.] "Any opposed?" [Pause, record any.] "Any abstentions?" [Pause, record any.]

"[NAME] is elected as [POSITION]."

[Repeat for each officer position. If you are being nominated as President/Board Chair, ask another board member to facilitate that vote.]

"Our officers are now [NAME] as President/Board Chair, [NAME] as Secretary, and [NAME] as Treasurer. Congratulations to each of you."

Note: You cannot preside over your own election. Ask another board member to run the vote for President/Board Chair.
5
Conflict of interest disclosures and signatures
Action required
Say this

"Board members were asked to complete the conflict of interest disclosure form in advance of today's meeting. Thank you to those who did. The Conflict of Interest Policy is included as an appendix to our newly adopted bylaws."

📄 If anyone hasn't submitted the COI form yet: "If you haven't already submitted your conflict of interest disclosure, please open the COI Disclosure Form in the board portal now — it's under Board Meetings → Meeting Packet. You can complete and submit it from there."
Continue

[If anyone has not yet completed the form, give them a moment to do so now.]

"I'd like to confirm for the record — does anyone have a conflict of interest to disclose at this time, either from the form or arising now?"

[If yes, follow the recusal process in the bylaws. Note the disclosure and the recusal in the minutes. If no:]

"No conflicts disclosed. The signed forms will be retained in the Foundation's permanent records."

Before the meeting: Review all submitted forms for any disclosures so you are prepared to handle them on the day. Secretary: Note in the minutes that conflict of interest disclosures were completed by all directors and state whether any conflicts were disclosed.

Wants to table it: "We can table it, but keep in mind the handbook governs how we operate going forward — I'd like us to have it in place today if at all possible. Can we adopt as presented and commit to a revision at the next meeting?"

If someone disagrees or wants a change

Concern about the signatory: "We can adjust the motion to name a different signatory — or add a second one if we'd like dual signatures required. What's the board's preference?" [Update the motion language accordingly and proceed to vote.]

Concern about the bank: "The resolution authorizes opening an account — it doesn't lock us into a specific bank. The treasurer will research the best option. Comfortable proceeding on that basis?"

9
Resolution: spending authority threshold
Vote required
Say this

"Our board policies establish that the treasurer may approve expenditures below a dollar threshold set by board resolution without additional board approval. We need to set that threshold today."

"I propose a threshold of $500. This means the treasurer can approve individual expenditures up to $500 without a board vote, as long as they fall within approved budget categories. Anything above that requires board approval. Does anyone have thoughts on this number?"

[Allow brief discussion. If the board agrees on $500, proceed. If they propose a different amount, substitute it in the motion below.]

Motion language

"I move that the Board of Directors set the treasurer's spending authority threshold at $500 per expenditure, effective immediately."

After motion is seconded, say this

"All in favor — please raise your hand." [Count and record hands.] "Any opposed?" [Pause, record any.] "Any abstentions?" [Pause, record any.]

"The motion carries. The spending threshold is set at $500."

If someone wants a different amount

"Totally open to that — what number feels right to the board?" [Take suggestions, land on a number the group agrees on, substitute it in the motion, and proceed to vote.]

If they want a higher threshold: Common ranges for small nonprofits are $500–$2,500. Any amount is fine — just update the motion.

If they want a lower threshold: "We can absolutely go lower — just keep in mind the treasurer will need board approval for anything above that number, so a very low threshold means more votes for routine expenses."
