Website review checklist

Board approval draft  ·  setd5syndrome.org  ·  May 2026

Complete this checklist before the board meets to discuss and approve the website for full public launch. Each board member should visit setd5syndrome.org and review the site before the meeting.

Section 1

Page-by-page review

  • Home — headline, placeholder quote, and calls to action reviewed for accuracy and tone
  • About the Foundation — mission statement, board bios, SAB and MAB sections reviewed
  • Research — research priorities and framing of the Foundation's role reviewed
  • Community — Connect Directly form, external resources list, and Share Your Story section reviewed
  • Donate — 501(c)(3) pending notice, interim support messaging, and notify-me signup reviewed
  • Contact — contact form, email address, and response time language reviewed
  • All inaccurate or incomplete content identified and flagged for correction before launch
  • All content approved for public distribution
Section 2

Logo and branding

  • Board formally approves the current logo as the Foundation's official logo
  • Logo displays consistently and correctly across all pages of the site
  • Decision made on logo usage guidelines (minimum size, approved color versions, prohibited modifications) — or deferred to future meeting
  • Custodian of master logo files designated

Section 4

Scientific Advisory Board

The website includes a SAB section on the About page stating the Foundation is assembling the board and welcoming inquiries from researchers and scientists. No individual members are listed yet.

Current website copy — SAB section "We are in the process of assembling our Scientific Advisory Board. If you are a researcher or scientist with expertise relevant to SETD5 Syndrome, rare disease genetics, neurodevelopment, or related fields, we would welcome a conversation."
  • Board approves the SAB as a formal body of the Foundation
  • SAB website copy reviewed and approved
  • Point of contact for SAB recruitment and relationship management designated
  • Decision made on SAB inquiry contact method (general contact form or dedicated email)
Section 5

Medical Advisory Board

The website includes a MAB section on the About page welcoming interest from physicians, geneticists, neurologists, and other clinicians. No individual members are listed yet.

Current website copy — MAB section "We are building our Medical Advisory Board and welcome interest from physicians, geneticists, neurologists, and other clinicians who work with families navigating rare neurodevelopmental diagnoses."
  • Board approves the MAB as a formal body of the Foundation
  • MAB website copy reviewed and approved
  • Point of contact for MAB recruitment designated
  • Decision made on whether SAB and MAB will eventually have formal bylaws or terms of service, and if so, who will lead that process
Section 6

Research priorities

The website lists four research priorities. The board should confirm these represent the Foundation's current focus before they are published publicly.

  • Natural History Study
  • Patient & Variant Registry
  • Family & Caregiver Quality of Life Study
  • Clinical Care Guidelines
Current website copy — Research section "Our role is to support the researchers doing the work — connecting with investigators and raising the funds to make each of these a reality."
  • Board approves the four stated research priorities as the Foundation's current public focus
  • Natural history study researcher relationship discussed and next steps identified
  • No additional priorities need to be added at this time
Section 7

Community page

The Community page includes three sections: a direct family connection pathway, curated external resources, and an invitation for families to share their story.

  • Connect Directly — families can fill out a form to be connected with other SETD5 Syndrome families
  • Find Your Community — curated links to external organizations, with a disclaimer that the Foundation does not formally endorse any external organization
  • Share Your Story — an invitation for families to contribute their story to the Foundation's work
Disclaimer on the site: "The resources listed below are provided for informational purposes only. The Foundation does not formally endorse any external organization, website, or community. Families are encouraged to evaluate resources independently."
  • Board approves the Community page and its three sections
  • External organizations list reviewed and approved (review directly on the site)
  • Point of contact for Connect Directly form responses and family introductions designated
  • No external resources need to be added or removed at this time
Section 8

Donate page

The donation page currently reflects that the Foundation's 501(c)(3) application is pending with the IRS. The page states clearly that donations are not yet being accepted and that giving will not be tax-deductible until tax-exempt status is confirmed. Visitors can sign up to be notified when giving opens.

Current status: The Foundation is not actively processing donations. The page includes a notify-me email signup and a preview section — "Once Our 501(c)(3) Is Confirmed" — that outlines planned giving options including one-time and recurring donations, employer matching, in-kind support, and planned giving.
  • 501(c)(3) pending messaging reviewed and approved for public display
  • Notify-me email signup confirmed working and responses routed to the right inbox
  • Decision made on which donation platform to activate once 501(c)(3) is approved (e.g., GiveButter, Zeffy, PayPal Giving Fund)
  • Decision made on whether to offer any way for supporters to contribute now — or confirmed that the board is comfortable waiting for full 501(c)(3) approval
  • Plan in place for updating the donate page promptly once 501(c)(3) approval arrives
Section 9

Social media

The website currently shows Facebook and Instagram as "Coming Soon." The board should decide on timing and ownership before or at the meeting.

  • Social media launch timing decided
  • Responsibility for managing social accounts assigned
  • Decision made on whether social launch is tied to website launch or handled separately
Section 10

Photos and privacy

The website features photos of children and families living with SETD5 Syndrome. The board should confirm consent is in place for current photos and establish a policy for obtaining photos going forward.

Why this matters: Many families in the SETD5 Syndrome community are cautious about public identification of their children due to disability-related stigma, insurance concerns, and personal preference. A clear, opt-in consent process protects families and the Foundation.
Current photos on the site
  • All photos currently on the site have explicit written permission for public, indefinite use
  • All families whose child appears on the site have been informed that their photo is on a publicly accessible website
  • No photos need to be removed or replaced before launch
Obtaining more photos
  • Point of contact for future photo requests designated
  • Process for requesting photos decided (direct email, website form, or community channels)
  • Decision made on whether to collect more photos before launch or after
  • Any pages or sections that feel thin on photos identified and prioritized
Policy decisions
  • Board approves creating a formal photo consent form for website, social media, and printed materials use
  • Decision made on whether families can specify how their photos may be used (e.g., website only, newsletter only, not social media)
  • Decision made on whether photo consent will be time-limited and require renewal
  • Process for handling photo removal requests established
  • Responsibility for tracking photo consent assigned
  • Privacy policy updated to explicitly address photo consent — or update scheduled before launch
Section 11

Contact and email

The contact form sends messages to info@setd5syndrome.org. This inbox should be actively monitored once the site is live.

  • Responsibility for monitoring info@setd5syndrome.org and how often confirmed
  • Decision made on whether to state a response time on the site (e.g., "we respond within 3 business days")
