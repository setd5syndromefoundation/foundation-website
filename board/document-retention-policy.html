1 Purpose and scope

This Document Retention and Destruction Policy establishes minimum retention periods for records created or received by the SETD5 Syndrome Foundation (the "Foundation") and prohibits the destruction of documents relevant to any pending or reasonably anticipated legal matter, government inquiry, or audit. This policy applies to all board members, officers, committee members, and volunteers acting on behalf of the Foundation.

2 General principles
  • Records shall be retained for at least the minimum periods specified in the Retention Schedule in Section 4.
  • No record subject to a pending or anticipated legal matter, audit, or government inquiry may be destroyed, regardless of its scheduled retention period. The board president will issue a litigation hold notice when such a situation arises.
  • Electronic and paper records are subject to the same retention requirements.
  • The board portal serves as the Foundation's primary document repository. Records stored there are considered official Foundation records.
  • Records containing sensitive personal information about families, donors, or research participants shall be handled with appropriate care and disposed of securely when retention periods expire.
3 Responsibility

The secretary is responsible for maintaining governance records. The treasurer is responsible for financial and tax records. Committee chairs are responsible for records generated within their committee's work. The board president oversees overall compliance with this policy.

Any board member who becomes aware of a pending or threatened legal matter, government inquiry, or audit must immediately notify the board president. The board president will issue a litigation hold suspending destruction of all potentially relevant records until the matter is resolved.

5 Storage and security

All official Foundation records shall be stored in the board portal or in a secure physical location designated by the board. Records containing personally identifiable information about families, donors, or research participants shall be stored in a password-protected environment with access limited to those with a need to know. Board members are responsible for ensuring that Foundation records in their possession are stored securely and not shared outside authorized channels.

6 Destruction of records

Records that have met their minimum retention period and are not subject to a litigation hold may be destroyed. Paper records containing sensitive information shall be shredded. Electronic records shall be permanently deleted. The secretary shall maintain a brief log of records destroyed, including the record type, date range, and date of destruction.

The following records may never be destroyed:

  • Articles of Incorporation and all amendments
  • Bylaws and all amendments
  • IRS determination letter and Form 1023
  • All IRS Form 990 filings
  • All board meeting minutes and written consent votes
7 Amendments

This policy may be amended by a majority vote of the board at any duly noticed meeting or by written consent. The secretary will update the board portal with the current version upon adoption of any amendment.
